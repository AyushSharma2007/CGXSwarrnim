## A] Arithmatic Operators

### 1. Addition `+`

1. What is the output of `15 + 27`?  
2. Calculate the total price if a book costs ₹350 and a pen costs ₹45.  
3. What is the result of `"25" + 10` and why?  
4. A person has ₹2000 in their wallet. They buy items worth ₹750 and ₹320. Write an expression using `+` to find the total spent, then calculate the remaining balance.  
5. Predict the outputs and explain:  
   ```js
   console.log(5 + "5" + 5);
   console.log(5 + 5 + "5");
   console.log("5" + 5 + 5);
   ```

---

### 2. Subtraction `-`

1. What is the output of `100 - 37`?  
2. A tank has 500 litres of water. After using 175 litres, how much water is left?  
3. What is the result of `"50" - 20` and `"50" - "20"`? Explain any difference.  
4. A shopkeeper had 240 apples. He sold 95 in the morning and 67 in the evening. Write expressions to find how many apples are left.  
5. Predict and explain the outputs:  
   ```js
   console.log("100" - 50);
   console.log("abc" - 10);
   console.log(10 - "5" - "2");
   console.log("10" - "5" - "2");
   ```

---

### 3. Multiplication `*`

1. What is the output of `12 * 8`?  
2. One pizza costs ₹299. What is the total cost of 4 pizzas?  
3. What is the result of `"7" * 6` and `"7" * "6"`?  
4. A factory produces 45 units per hour. How many units does it produce in 8 hours? Write the expression and calculate.  
5. Predict and explain the outputs:  
   ```js
   console.log("5" * 3 * "2");
   console.log("abc" * 4);
   console.log(10 * "2.5");
   console.log("10" * "2.5" * "0");
   ```

---

### 4. Division `/`

1. What is the output of `144 / 12`?  
2. 360 students are to be divided equally into 9 classrooms. How many students per classroom?  
3. What is the result of `"100" / 4` and `"100" / "4"`?  
4. A total bill of ₹2400 is to be shared equally among 6 friends. Write the expression and find each person’s share.  
5. Predict and explain the outputs:  
   ```js
   console.log(10 / 0);
   console.log(-10 / 0);
   console.log(0 / 0);
   console.log("20" / "4" / 2);
   console.log("abc" / 5);
   ```

---

### 5. Modulus `%`

1. What is the output of `29 % 5`?  
2. There are 23 chocolates to be packed in boxes of 4. How many chocolates will be left over?  
3. What is the result of `0 % 7` and `15 % 0`? Explain.  
4. A number of pages (47) needs to be printed on sheets that hold 6 pages each. How many full sheets are needed and how many pages will be left over? Write expressions using `%` and `/`.  
5. Predict and explain the outputs (especially the signs):  
   ```js
   console.log(17 % 5);
   console.log(-17 % 5);
   console.log(17 % -5);
   console.log(-17 % -5);
   console.log(10 % 0);
   ```

---

### 6. Exponentiation `**`

1. What is the output of `3 ** 4`?  
2. Calculate the area of a square whose side is 9 units using the exponentiation operator.  
3. What is the result of `2 ** 5` and `5 ** 2`? Are they the same?  
4. Predict and explain the outputs (and any errors):  
   ```js
   console.log(2 ** 3 ** 2);          // right-associative
   console.log((2 ** 3) ** 2);
   console.log(2 ** -3);
   // console.log(-2 ** 2);           // Why does this cause a SyntaxError?
   console.log((-2) ** 2);
   console.log(4 ** 0.5);
   ```
